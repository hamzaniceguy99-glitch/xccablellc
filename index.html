How we work

Low voltage only, and we say so

XCable LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations. Low-voltage work is carried out under the applicable Florida licence, which appears on our quotations.

We don't do line-voltage electrical work — that's a licensed electrical contractor's job and we'll refer you to one. We also don't sell alarm monitoring or network security services. What we do is install, test and document the physical infrastructure properly.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Do you do electrical work too?

No. We're low voltage. Line-voltage work goes to a licensed electrical contractor, and we'll coordinate with yours or recommend one.

Will you test the cabling?

Every run, as part of the job, with the results handed to you. A visual check isn't a test.

Can you work around our business hours?

Yes — evenings and weekends by arrangement, which is often easier for occupied offices.

Do you supply the cameras and switches?

We can advise, and we're happy to install equipment you've chosen. The infrastructure is our job; configuration is usually your IT provider's.

Are you insured?

Yes, and details are available on request before work begins.

What about permits?

Obtained where the applicable code requires them, and included in the quote so there are no surprises.

Got cabling to price?

A free site survey and a fixed written quote, with no obligation.
