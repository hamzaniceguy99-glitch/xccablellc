## Business description (Stripe, directories…)

```
XCable LLC, a Florida limited liability company, installs structured cabling and low-voltage systems for commercial and residential clients: data cabling and fibre networks, cabling for cameras, access control and audio-visual systems, rack and equipment room build-outs, and certification testing with results documented. Work is quoted in writing after a free site survey and carried out under the applicable Florida licensing for low-voltage work, with permits obtained where required. The company does not carry out line-voltage electrical work, which is referred to licensed electrical contractors. Site: xccablellc.shop
```
